# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

Be respectful, constructive, precise, and empathetic. Accept feedback gracefully. Focus criticism
on ideas and evidence. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and
publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation for behavior they reasonably consider inappropriate. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository owner. Maintainers will respect reporter privacy and apply a
proportionate, consistent response.

This compact policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles.
